3. Cancellation Prior to Document Review

If you submit a cancellation ticket before our compliance expert group conducts data validation checks, structural document reviews, or name reservation audits, you are eligible for a complete refund of your order track fee, minus a small processing merchant transaction gateway surcharge fee of 10%.

4. Cancellation During active Preparation Stage

If your application has been verified, matched, and internal document sets have been drawn up or compiled, but the paperwork has not been officially submitted to state departments or corporate registries, you are eligible for a partial refund. We will return your payment minus a $50.00 standard administrative execution charge to compensate for internal compliance hours.

5. Non-Refundable Status Post-Submission

Once filings4u agents have submitted your data fields, signed paperwork, digital proxies, or registration credentials to any local state authority, court, the IRS, or federal logistics registries (e.g., FMCSA, UCR, or IFTA platforms), **no refunds will be granted under any circumstance**. The funds at this phase have already been permanently moved to administrative bodies.

6. State Backlogs and Processing Surcharges

State administrative backlogs, department delays, or government network outages are entirely outside the operational jurisdiction of filings4u, LLC. Cancellations or refunds demanded on the grounds of slow government turnaround phases post-submission will be rejected.

7. How to Submit a Refund Request

To file an official request, users must open an application ticket through the client portal or send a direct email to our compliance oversight committee. All inquiries must list your primary transaction reference code, chosen deployment package, and company name fields:

filings4u, LLC — Intake Desk
Email: refunds@filings4u.com
Legal Handling HQ: State of Illinois

8. Processing Surcharges and Gateway Timelines

Approved partial or full refunds will be credited back via the exact merchant credit card loop or secure payment gateway token used during checkout. Financial banking loops usually take 5 to 7 business processing days to balance accounts.

9. Governing Jurisdiction

This Refund and Cancellation Policy is executed and interpreted in accordance with the business codes and state laws of the State of Illinois.
